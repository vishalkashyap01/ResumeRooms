STAR bullet pattern

Illustrative example, not your data:

BeforeResponsible for sales reports.
AfterBuilt a weekly Power BI sales dashboard (Action) for a 12-person team (Situation/Task), cutting reporting time by 40% (Result).

Formula: strong verb + what you did + tool + measurable result. Only claim numbers you can defend in an interview.
